# Sources

Shared source records. Code and data refer to a source by its id. Standards
are cited by designation and scope only (see [data_policy.md](data_policy.md)).

| Id | Kind | Reference | Used for | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SRC-SHIGLEY | book | Budynas, R. G. and Nisbett, J. K., *Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design*. Edition and equation numbers: verify against owner's copy. | N2, F1–F6, M1–M4, B2, C1–C3 | cited (verify) |

Every relation that uses `SRC-SHIGLEY` carries `verify=True` in its result
note until the owner has checked the edition, the equation numbers and the
coefficients against their own copy.
