# 0002 — Phase 3 decisions

Status: accepted

## Decisions

- **D9 — K_f on the mean stress:** the fatigue stress-concentration factor
  K_f is applied to both σ_a and σ_m. `mean_stress.assess` takes the notched
  stresses.
- **Local-yield warning:** if K_f |σ_max| > S_y (σ_max the largest absolute
  nominal stress of the cycle), the result note warns "local yielding at
  notch; mean-stress treatment needs review (verify)". The safety factors are
  still reported.
- **Life range warning:** Basquin constants carry their valid life range
  [N_min, N_max] and status. A computed N_f outside the range is kept and the
  note warns "outside the stated data range".
- **Textbook relations with verify flags:** N2, F1–F6, M1–M4, B2 and C1–C3
  are taken from SRC-SHIGLEY (docs/sources.md). They are marked "(verify)" in
  their docstrings and set verify flags in result notes until the owner has
  checked edition, equation numbers and coefficients.
- **Exact-acceleration deviation replaces the λ threshold:** the λ > 0.35
  verify flag had no source and is removed. `evaluate_cycle` still uses the
  series K3, and its note reports the largest deviation from the exact
  acceleration K3e on the evaluated grid.
- **Pin and ring masses may be zero:** m_pin ≥ 0 and m_rings ≥ 0, because they
  are often lumped into the piston mass; m_piston and m_rod stay > 0.
