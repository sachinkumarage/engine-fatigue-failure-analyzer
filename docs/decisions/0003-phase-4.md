# 0003 — Phase 4 decisions

Status: accepted

## Decisions

- **Rod stress sign and axial-only model:** stress is tension positive and the
  rod force is compression positive, so the rod stress is σ = −F_rod / A (R1).
  Only axial stress is modelled; rod whip bending is excluded (D5). The whole
  F_rod is used at every section (conservative in tension).
- **Single-throw beam and X3:** the crankshaft is one throw between two main
  bearings, statically determinate and symmetric, with the output torque at
  the drive-side journal (D4). Statics gives T_pin = F_t r / 2 on both sides of
  the crankpin and T_j = F_t r at the drive-side journal; the derivation is in
  docs/methods/crankshaft.md.
- **Fillet point:** the assessed point lies in the crank plane at the fillet,
  on the pin side facing the crank axis. Bending from the tangential force is
  zero there (neutral axis), so only the radial bending moment and the torsion
  act. Separate K_f in bending and torsion (D4).
- **Separate S2 for σ and τ:** amplitude and mean are taken separately from
  the σ and τ histories and combined with von Mises (S3). Extrema of σ and τ
  are treated as simultaneous; non-proportional loading is not modelled.
- **Bolt load model:** the external load per big-end bolt is the most tensile
  rod force plus the centrifugal force of the rotating rod mass outside the
  cap, shared by n_b bolts (J7). Bolt fatigue uses J6 (Goodman along the
  preload load line) with a fully corrected bolt endurance limit instead of
  M1–M3; no Marin factors or K_f are applied again.
- **Finite life only when n_Goodman < 1:** Basquin/Morrow life (B2) is
  computed only for locations with n_Goodman < 1; otherwise the result states
  "no finite-life calculation (n ≥ 1)".
- **Governing item:** the lowest of fatigue n (Goodman, or M5 for compressive
  mean), Langer yield n and buckling n; for bolts the lowest of n_p, n_0, n_f.
