# Data policy

1. No invented material, load or test data presented as real.
2. Every material property carries source, date and status
   (`cited`, `measured`, `assumed`).
3. Measured data is stored with specimen id, method, instrument and date.
4. Raw research data (photographs, measurements of real failed parts) stays
   outside this repository; the repository uses synthetic or published
   example data only.
5. Standards are cited by designation and scope only, without years unless
   certain; no links to paywalled copies.
6. No secrets, no raw research data, nothing that identifies a vehicle, owner
   or workshop. CI fails if files under `data/` are committed.
